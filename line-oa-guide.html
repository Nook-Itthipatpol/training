01 / The foundations

What is LINE OA, really?

LINE Official Account is a business presence inside the LINE ecosystem. Unlike a personal LINE account, it combines customer conversations, business messaging, self-service tools, and analytics.

START HERE ↘
↗

Acquire

Get customers to add your account using a QR code, friend-add link, website, or physical point of sale.

Aim: relevant followers
☏

Serve

Answer questions, route people to services, and offer a helpful experience through chat and rich menus.

Aim: less customer effort
◎

Retain

Share useful updates, promotions, reminders when appropriate, and loyalty benefits to encourage repeat visits.

Aim: ongoing customer value
The most important mental model

LINE OA is not just a broadcasting channel. It is a lightweight customer relationship and service hub. Start with the customer’s job to be done—not the features you want to use.

04 / The messaging playbook

Less noise. More value.

Use short, specific, action-oriented messages. Below are editable examples you can copy and adapt.

01. Welcome / ข้อความทักทาย

Hi 👋 Welcome to [Business Name]! We're here to help with [main service]. Tap “Book / Shop” below to get started, or reply with your question. Our team responds during [hours].

Why it works: tells the customer what the account does, what to do now, and when a person will reply.

02. Useful broadcast / บรอดแคสต์

A quick update from [Business Name] ✨ [One clear benefit or useful announcement] Available: [dates / conditions] See details or book here: [link] Questions? Just reply to this chat.

Why it works: one message, one main promise, visible terms, and a clear next step.

03. FAQ auto-reply / ตอบกลับอัตโนมัติ

Thanks for contacting [Business Name]. We're open [days and hours]. Location: [map link] Bookings: [link] If this doesn't answer your question, type “STAFF” and our team will follow up during service hours.

Why it works: resolves repeat questions but does not trap people in automation.

04. Follow-up / ติดตามผล

Hi [Name], thank you for visiting [Business Name]. Was everything clear and helpful today? You can reply here if you need support. We also welcome your feedback: [link]

Why it works: focuses on service quality. Send individual follow-ups only with an appropriate basis and workflow.

✓ Good habits

  • Deliver a useful reason to stay subscribed.
  • Use audience targeting where available and appropriate.
  • Preview on a real mobile device before sending.
  • Set realistic response times and clear escalation.
  • Compare results against a defined campaign goal.

× Avoid these

  • Broadcasting the same sales pitch to everyone.
  • Overusing urgency or misleading promotions.
  • Promising an instant human response you cannot provide.
  • Letting auto-replies loop or contradict staff.
  • Sending personal or sensitive details in bulk messages.

06 / Responsible operations

Build trust into the workflow.

Good account operations are as important as content. Define ownership, privacy practices, and escalation rules before you grow.

✓

Daily operating rhythm

Assign a primary inbox owner and backup. Check open conversations, tag or categorize requests when supported, and close loops with customers. Keep a simple issue log for recurring questions.

◎

Weekly improvement rhythm

Review inquiries, response delays, campaign performance, blocked-user trends, and frequently used menu options. Update one process or message based on what you learned.

♧

Privacy by design

Explain why you collect customer information, use appropriate consent or another lawful basis, respect preferences, restrict staff access, and follow Thailand’s PDPA and your organization’s data policies.

!

Automate carefully

Test greetings, keyword replies, staff handoff, and business-hour routing end to end. Keep integrations and API work separate from basic manager features until you have a clear use case.

!
Extra care for healthcare, clinics, and other sensitive services

Do not use broad promotional broadcasts to reveal someone’s health status, diagnoses, appointment details, or other sensitive information. For medical or confidential matters, use an organization-approved secure process and appropriate access controls. Treat LINE OA as a service touchpoint, not automatically as a compliant medical-record system. This guide is not legal or clinical compliance advice.
